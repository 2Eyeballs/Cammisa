About

Cammisa Buerhaus is a theatre artist, writer, and visual artist. For the past 10 years Buerhaus has worked between artistic disciplines, developing a hybrid practice across harsh noise, performance, and visual art. Buerhaus writes and directs confessional-performative psychodramas written in her signature hallucinatory prose.

She has recently exhibited at Kim(?) Contemporary Art Center, Riga, Latvia (2026), CHB Fine Arts, Berlin, DE (2026), and OSMOS, NYC (2025). 2026 performances include Buerhaus's dramatic monologues, Bookwoman at kammer in Hamburg, DE and The Maze in the historical Royal Suite of Hotel Adlon, Berlin, DE, as well as roles in Erika Landstrom's play Desintegration, Mies van der Rohe Haus, Berlin, DE and Christiane Huber's Pool People, Munich Public Art Focus Year, Munich, DE.

Buerhaus has acted in productions by Richard Maxwell, Martine Syms, Jordan Strafer, among others. She is the subject and actor of a 2027 experimental documentary by Rachel Ashton.
